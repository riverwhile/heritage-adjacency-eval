# Nerfacto alternative-position subset provenance

Nerfacto blocked-v2 and blocked-v3 use a fixed four-object subset:

- `bronze_cup`
- `bronze_statue`
- `silver_comb`
- `stone_horse`

The subset was inherited from the earlier 3DGS block-position sensitivity run and was chosen purposively to include bronze, silver-coloured metal, and stone. It excludes the textile and ceramic objects.

The retained project record names the four objects for the 3DGS extension on 12 August 2026 and records their reuse for Nerfacto on 15 August 2026, after inspection of the seven-object interleaved and blocked-v1 comparison. The record therefore supports a fixed inherited subset, but not an independently preregistered validation sample or the earliest date on which the material-diversity rationale was formulated.

The 16-model matrix contains four objects, two capture conditions, and two alternative block positions. It is an exploratory position-sensitivity extension and should not be interpreted as a representative seven-object replication.
